# World Overview

This is a multi-layered epic fantasy centered around the laws of reality, interdimensional contamination, and the survival of civilization.

The universe is divided into four independent realms: the Divine Realm, the Mortal Realm, the Demon Realm, and the Spirit Realm. These worlds were never meant to interact. Each realm exists according to its own fundamental laws, separated by boundaries that preserve the stability of reality itself.

Crossing those boundaries is considered a violation of the world's natural order. Every interdimensional intervention leaves invisible scars upon reality. Over countless ages, those scars accumulated into something far more terrifying:

**The Void**.

The Void is neither a god nor a living creature. It is the inevitable consequence of reality collapsing under repeated violations of its own laws—a cosmic phenomenon that reduces everything to absolute **Zero**.

---

# The First Cataclysm

Long before humanity ruled the world, dragons stood at the pinnacle of civilization while other races lived beneath their dominion.

Everything changed when a dying being fell from the heavens like a meteor.

Known only as the **Evil God**, it was not native to this universe. It originated from a higher dimension, possibly having been defeated by an even greater deity before crashing into this world.

The Dragon Queen discovered the wounded god and chose to save it.

What began as compassion eventually became love.

Their union produced descendants carrying the blood of both dragons and the Evil God.

But the Evil God's nature was fundamentally incompatible with the world.

It embodied domination, chaos, and endless conflict, seeking to reshape existence into a realm where only the strongest deserved to survive.

Its ambition ignited the First Cataclysm.

As civilization collapsed and hope faded, countless beings prayed for salvation.

Those prayers reached beyond the boundaries of reality itself.

For the first time, another higher-dimensional existence crossed into the world.

The being later worshipped as the **God of Light** descended together with the divine race.

After a devastating war, the Evil God and the dragon empire were defeated.

Humanity rose from the ashes and entered its first golden age.

Some humans even united with members of the divine race, giving birth to the first generation of **Demigods**.

---

# The Origin of the Void

Although the world had been saved, the damage had already been done.

The arrival of the Evil God created the first fracture.

The descent of the God of Light widened it further.

Every future attempt to travel between realms deepened those wounds.

As centuries passed, every civilization sought to exploit interdimensional travel for its own benefit.

Reality gradually became contaminated.

From those accumulated fractures, the Void was born.

Unlike gods, demons, or mortals, the Void possesses no consciousness, no morality, and no ambition.

It is simply the inevitable end of a broken universe.

Where the Void spreads, existence itself is erased.

Not destroyed.

Not consumed.

Simply reduced to **Zero**.

---

# The Second Cataclysm

Six hundred and sixty-six years after the First Cataclysm, another disaster engulfed the world.

One of the divine race fell from grace.

Banished from heaven, the outcast became the first **Demon King**, gathering the forces of the Demon Realm to wage war against every civilization.

The conflict nearly ended the world once again.

At humanity's darkest hour, descendants carrying ancient bloodlines awakened extraordinary powers inherited from forgotten ages.

Together with the divine race, they defeated the Demon King.

The Evil God, whose influence still lingered, was divided into six fragments and sealed away across the world.

Peace returned.

But only temporarily.

---

# The Third Cataclysm

The true story begins before the Third Cataclysm.

During the era of the First Cataclysm, a gifted archangel possessed the ability to predict countless possible futures.

Again and again, every path ended in the same conclusion.

The world would perish.

He could never see the exact cause of the apocalypse.

Only its certainty.

From that moment onward, he devoted his entire existence to preventing the inevitable future.

Yet while civilizations believed themselves safe, the fractures between worlds continued to expand.

The Void was already spreading beneath reality itself.

The final catastrophe had already begun.

---

# The Four Great Legends

Throughout history, four legends have existed beyond the reach of ordinary civilization.

## The Divine Necropolis

Far beyond the northern frozen sea lies the hidden homeland of the Demigods.

They do not rule the world.

Instead, they observe it from afar, acting as silent guardians of balance.

Every three centuries, they choose a single champion to protect their civilization.

The current guardian is an ice-wielding Demigod regarded as the strongest of his generation.

---

## Dragon Isle

To the west stands an enormous prison island where the descendants of dragons and the Evil God remain sealed.

Many inhabitants still carry the blood of the ancient Evil God.

It is one of the most dangerous places in existence, where the remnants of the First Cataclysm continue to survive.

---

## The Man of the Void

In the south appears an enigmatic figure known only as **The Man of the Void**.

Bound by countless chains, he emerges wherever large Void fractures open.

His arrival brings indiscriminate destruction.

When the fracture eventually closes, the chains drag him back into the Void once again.

No one knows whether he is a prisoner, a guardian, or something even older than the Void itself.

---

## The Twilight Troupe

From the east travels a legendary wandering circus known as the **Twilight Troupe**.

Unlike ordinary performers, they represent the very concept of **Infinity**.

If the Void symbolizes absolute Zero—the inevitable end of all existence—then the Twilight Troupe embodies limitless possibility, imagination, creativity, and evolution.

Nothing is impossible within their performances.

Shadows become actors.

Blood becomes art.

Suicide becomes theater.

Monsters become comedians.

Their existence appears irrational only because they refuse to be constrained by conventional reality.

They do not oppose the laws of the world through force.

They transcend them through imagination.

Where the Void erases all possibilities, the Twilight Troupe creates infinite new ones.

---

# The Final Conflict

As the Third Cataclysm approaches, the four fundamental forces governing existence will finally converge.

**Order.**

**Chaos.**

**Nothingness.**

**Infinity.**

Each represents a different answer to the fate of reality.

Each believes itself to be the future of the world.

Only one can remain when the final curtain falls.
